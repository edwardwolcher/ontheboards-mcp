# Privacy Policy

**OnTheBoards MCP**
**Last updated: February 24, 2026**

## 1. Overview

OnTheBoards MCP is an internal integration tool used by On the Boards, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit performing arts organization in Seattle, Washington. This policy describes how data is handled by this application.

## 2. Data Accessed

This application accesses QuickBooks Online accounting data belonging to On the Boards, including but not limited to accounts, transactions, journal entries, vendors, and customers.

## 3. Data Use

All data accessed through this application is used solely for On the Boards' internal accounting and financial management purposes. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.

## 4. Data Storage

OAuth credentials (access tokens and refresh tokens) are stored locally on authorized staff devices. No QuickBooks data is stored permanently outside of QuickBooks Online itself.

## 5. Data Sharing

On the Boards does not share QuickBooks data accessed through this application with any third party, except as required by law or as necessary for audit and tax compliance.

## 6. Security

Access is restricted to authorized personnel. OAuth tokens are stored in secured local files and are not transmitted to any external service beyond Intuit's QuickBooks API.
